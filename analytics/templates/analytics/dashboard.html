Crime insights

No double counting. SAPS publishes individual crimes and subtotals (for example "Contact crime" is the sum of murder, rape and others). Only individual crimes are added up here.

Crimes included. "Community-reported serious crime" is SAPS's headline measure. "All recorded crime" also adds crimes detected by police action (such as drug offences, which rise when police search more) and kidnapping.

Change compares the same quarter one year earlier, because crime is seasonal. Murder rate uses SAPS's population estimates (April–June); for other quarters it is approximate.
